This subsection demonstrates how to present a regression specification. The
generic linear model below is unrelated to any research topic:

\begin{equation}
y_i = \alpha + \beta \, x_i + \mu_j + \mathbf{Z}_i'
\boldsymbol{\gamma} + \varepsilon_i ,
\label{eq:regression-1}
\end{equation}

where $y_i$ is the outcome for unit $i$, $x_i$ is the key variable defined
in equation~\eqref{eq:example-3}, $\mu_j$ are group fixed effects, and
$\mathbf{Z}_i$ is a vector of controls. Standard errors are clustered at the
group level.
